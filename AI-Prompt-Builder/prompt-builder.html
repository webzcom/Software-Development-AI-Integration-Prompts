Persona

Who is the AI playing?

Define the role, expertise level, and personality the AI should adopt. A well-defined persona shapes the tone, vocabulary, and depth of every response.

Preview

2

Context

What does it need to know?

Background information, constraints, and situational details that frame the request. More relevant context = fewer assumptions = better output.

3

Task

What exactly do you want?

Be precise. The best tasks use an action verb and describe the exact deliverable. Vague tasks produce vague outputs.

4

Permission

What should it do when uncertain?

Explicitly tell the AI what to do when it doesn't have enough information. This prevents hallucination and keeps outputs trustworthy.

5

Format

How should the output be structured?

Output format directly affects how usable the response is. Be explicit — the AI can't read your mind about whether you want markdown, a table, JSON, or flowing prose.

Prompt tips

        
    
          
  • Start with a persona — it immediately improves tone and vocabulary.
    • 
          
  • Context before task — always set the scene before asking for action.
    • 
          
  • Use a specific action verb: "analyze" beats "look at".
    • 
          
  • Tell the AI what to do when uncertain to prevent hallucination.
    • 
          
  • Specify output format or you'll get whatever the model defaults to.
